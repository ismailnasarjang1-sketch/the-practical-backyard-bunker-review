Editorial & Disclosure Policy

Who we are

The Practical Backyard Bunker Review is an independent informational website about the book The Practical Backyard Bunker by Christopher Mackenzie. Unless stated otherwise, we are not the author, publisher or seller.

How we write reviews

Pages are based on publicly available information about the product. We separate what a product claims from what we have verified, and we say clearly when we have not read or tested something.

Ratings and testimonials

We do not publish invented star ratings, fake reviews or made-up testimonials. Review structured data on this site contains no numeric rating unless one is based on a real, documented evaluation.

Links to the author's website are currently plain outbound links. If we join an affiliate program, links will be marked as sponsored and this page will be updated before they go live.

Safety and legal information

Information about shelters, excavation and construction is general only. It is not engineering, legal or safety advice. Follow local codes, obtain required permits, have utilities marked, and use licensed professionals for any real build.
